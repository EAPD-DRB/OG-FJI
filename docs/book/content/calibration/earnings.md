# Earnings

Fiji does not yet have the longitudinal earnings microdata needed to estimate
age-by-ability profiles directly. OG-FJI therefore starts with OG-USA's
normalized lifecycle profiles and applies the country-model family's scalar
tilt until the model-implied inequality ratio matches Fiji.

The target is an **income** Gini of 36.7 from the 2013–14 Household Income and
Expenditure Survey, reported in Fiji's
[2019 Voluntary National Review](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/3866768/files/Fiji_VNR_2019_final.pdf).
This older estimate is used because it matches the income concept of the
OG-USA 41.5 anchor. The newer 30.7 figure is consumption inequality and is not
substituted into an earnings calibration.

The final matrix is normalized so its population-weighted mean is one. This is
a distributional calibration; it does not claim that the U.S. lifecycle shape
is itself estimated from Fiji.
